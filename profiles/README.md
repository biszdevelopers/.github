## About Us

BISZ Developers' Club is a student-led technology club focused on artificial intelligence and practical software engineering. We build custom AI-powered solutions for students and student-led organizations at BISZ, and release open source tools for the broader community.

---

## What We Do

- **Custom AI Solutions** — Build tailored tools for real problems faced by students and student-led organizations, from automating workflows to intelligent assistants
- **Open Source Tools** — Release general-purpose utilities, models, and frameworks that anyone can use, modify, and contribute to
- **Collaborative Learning** — Work together on projects, share knowledge, and grow skills in machine learning, software engineering, and system design
- **Community Support** — Identify needs within the student body and deliver working software that makes daily tasks easier

---

## Our Mission

To make AI accessible and useful at BISZ. We believe the best way to learn is by building things that actually help people, so we take requests from students and student organizations and turn them into real, deployed solutions.

---

## Join Us

We welcome anyone with a genuine interest in technology to join. Whether you are already building things or just want to catch up to the age of AI, there is a place for you here.
